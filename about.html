About Me

I'm Josh Derrow (aka Slush). I have many interests including but not limited to: technology, music, gaming, development, hiking, art, weight lifting, hanging out with friends, & travel. I love building things of all sorts, and I especially enjoy projects where I can design and polish every detail myself. I also am really passionate about putting my own unique spin on things and this site is where all of that comes together.

Education

I graduated from JMU in May 2026 with a B.S. in Computer Science with minors in Mathematics and Computational Analytics. My CS and math studies at JMU (and even before that at AET) influenced how I think about and solve problems, as well as how I rationalize things in general. From algorithms to web development, my coursework pushed me to write programs that are clear, concise, tested, & built to last. I'm grateful for the professors who gave me a good foundation and made it stick, and I am especially grateful to Dr. John Chapin, Dr. John Bowers, Dr. Mike Lam, Dr. Paul Warne, & Dr. Chris Johnson. These professors specifically helped shape the programmer, the mathematician, & the overall person that I am today.
